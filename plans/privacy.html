Plans privacy policy

Effective October 15, 2026

Plans is a planning app from HIGHKEY Games LLC. This policy covers Plans for Apple devices, including its iCloud sync, shared Spaces, and Pro purchases.

Your plans and iCloud

Plans stores your Spaces, lists, tasks, schedules, notes, and completion history on your device. When iCloud is available, the app syncs this content through Apple's CloudKit private and shared databases. Your iCloud account manages access. HIGHKEY does not operate a server that receives this content or have access to your private iCloud database.

Plans uses iCloud participant identifiers and, where Apple provides them, participant names and email addresses to show collaborators and assign tasks. These details are used by the app and its iCloud sharing features, not for advertising.

Sharing a Space

When you share a Space, its content is stored in a CloudKit share accessible to the people you invite. Collaborators can see content and make changes according to their permissions. Their copies, exports, and backups may remain after you stop sharing.

The visibility settings for individual lists, plans, and schedules inside a shared Space control what the Plans interface shows. They do not create separate server access restrictions within that Space. Keep content that must be private from collaborators in a separate Space that you have not shared.

Contacts and Reminders

With your permission, Plans reads Contacts to find profile pictures for people in shared Spaces. It caches those pictures on your device. Plans does not upload your address book or contact pictures to HIGHKEY or to its CloudKit records. You can change Contacts access in your device's Settings. Previously cached pictures can remain in the app's local cache.

If you enable Reminders import, Plans asks for full Reminders access so it can read the lists you choose and move selected reminders into Plans. A successful import removes the original reminders from Apple Reminders. Imported titles, notes, and dates become Plans content and follow the same iCloud sync and sharing rules as other tasks. You can change Reminders access in Settings.

Backups, exports, and Live Activities

Plans creates backup files in its local Documents/Backups folder and, when available, in your iCloud Drive Plans/Backups folder. These files can contain the content and collaborator information in your Spaces. Automatic cleanup keeps files from the seven newest days that contain backups, plus one file from each of the four newest older weeks that contain backups. Older files are removed as newer backups replace them; this is not a fixed deletion period.

You can export a Space and choose where to save or send the file. The destination you select handles that copy under its own rules. Local and iCloud Drive backups are accessible in Files. Deleting an item in Plans does not delete its contents from existing exports or backups.

Live Activities can show the current task on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Consider who can see your device and adjust Live Activity access in Settings if needed.

Trial and purchases

Plans records the start of its 14-day trial in the device's Keychain. This record can remain after the app is deleted; it is not synced by Plans to iCloud. The app also stores preferences and purchase access state locally.

Apple processes Pro purchases and restores through StoreKit. Plans checks Apple's verified purchase information on your device to determine access. HIGHKEY does not receive your payment card details, and Plans does not send purchase receipts to a HIGHKEY server. Apple's account, purchase, and privacy terms apply to these transactions.

Analytics and advertising

Plans contains no advertising, third-party analytics, attribution, or crash-reporting SDK. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and HIGHKEY does not sell Plans data. Apple may provide App Store reports or diagnostics under Apple's services and your device's sharing choices.

Support and this website

If you email hello@highkeygames.com, HIGHKEY receives your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use that information to answer your request and resolve the issue. Please avoid sending private task content, contact details, or backups unless they are needed for your request. You can ask us to delete your support correspondence; we may retain information needed to meet legal obligations or resolve an ongoing dispute.

This policy and the Plans support page are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub processes web requests under its privacy statement, including technical information needed to serve and protect the website. These pages use no analytics scripts or advertising. Visiting the website is separate from storing your planning data in the app.

Managing and deleting your data

You can edit or delete content in Plans. Space owners can manage sharing or delete their Spaces; collaborators can leave shared Spaces. Changes sync when iCloud is available. Remove exported files and backups separately in Files and in any other destination where you saved them. Deleting the app alone does not remove iCloud data, external copies, or the trial record in Keychain.

You can also manage the app's iCloud storage through your Apple account settings. HIGHKEY cannot retrieve or delete your private iCloud content for you. Apple controls retention and recovery within its services. See Apple's privacy policy and GitHub's privacy statement for those services.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests, email hello@highkeygames.com. For app help, visit Plans support.

We will update this page and its effective date when Plans' data practices change.
