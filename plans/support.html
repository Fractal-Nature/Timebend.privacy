Plans support

Plans is a planning app from HIGHKEY Games LLC.

Get help

Email hello@highkeygames.com with “Plans support” in the subject. Include your device model, iOS version, and what happened. If you send a screenshot, hide private task or contact information first. Never send your Apple password or payment card details.

Start a plan

Create a Space on first launch. Use Plans to arrange tasks on a timeline and Lists to organize tasks by subject. Settings lets you manage Spaces and open Plans Pro.

Trial, Pro, and restoring a purchase

The 14-day trial starts in the app and does not automatically charge you. After it ends, your data stays available to view and export. Pro keeps editing available through a monthly or annual subscription, or a lifetime purchase. Open Settings, then Plans Pro to see the options and Restore Purchases.

Restore with the Apple account that made the purchase. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account's subscription settings. Deleting Plans does not cancel an Apple subscription. For billing or refund requests, use Apple's purchase support.

iCloud and shared Spaces

Check that iCloud and iCloud Drive are enabled for Plans in device Settings and that you have an internet connection. Sync may take time after reinstalling or accepting an invitation. Your devices should use the same iCloud account for your own content. Each collaborator uses their own iCloud account.

Share a Space only with people who should have access to its content. Per-list or per-plan visibility inside a shared Space is an app display setting, not a separate server access restriction. Keep private content in a separate unshared Space. See the privacy policy for details.

Backups and exports

Backups are saved in Files under On My iPhone or On My iPad, then Plans, then Backups. When iCloud Drive is available, a second copy is saved under iCloud Drive, Plans, Backups. Settings shows backup status and Space export/import controls. Export before making major changes. Deleting app content does not delete existing backup or exported files; remove those separately in Files if needed.

Reminders and contact photos

Reminders import moves selected reminders into Plans and deletes the original reminders after a successful import. Check the destination Space before importing into a shared Space. Contacts access is optional and supplies collaborator profile pictures. You can change either permission in device Settings.

Privacy

Read the Plans privacy policy, or send privacy requests to hello@highkeygames.com.
